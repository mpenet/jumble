# User guide

Jumble is your community's marketplace: a place to sell, give away and lend
things to each other, and to ask for what you are looking for. This guide covers everything members can do, then
what moderators do.

## Signing in

Every page of Jumble is for members only, except the privacy page.
Depending on how your community set it up, opening it either:

- asks for your email address and sends you a link: open it, choose
  **Sign in**, and you are in. A link works once, within 15 minutes; ask for
  another if it expired. If no email arrives, check your spam folder, or
  your address may not be one allowed in;
- or takes you to your community's sign-in page (Google, for instance).

Either way you come back to where you were going. You stay signed in on that
browser for a week after your last visit, and at most 30 days in a row;
**Sign out** in the header ends it at once.

If you are told your account is not allowed, the community limits Jumble to
some addresses or email domains: ask whoever runs it.

## The board

**Listings** shows everything on offer, newest first. Two sets of filters
narrow it down, and work together:

- **Type**: everything, for sale, free, to lend or wanted.
- **Category**: as set up by your community. Choosing a category again
  clears it. Only categories with something in them show; past the first
  ten, the others are behind **More categories**.

Each option shows how many listings it would give. The search box looks
through titles and descriptions as you type; every word must match, and the
last one may be the start of a word ("bik" finds "bike"). Results come best
match first. Filters, search and page are all in the address, so you can
share what you see as a link.

## Posting a listing

**Post a listing** opens the form:

- **You want to**: sell it, give it away, lend it, or look for it: a
  wanted post says what you need, without a price, and whoever has one
  answers with **I have one**, a message like any other.
- **Title**: up to 120 characters.
- **Price**: for sales only, in the community's currency.
- **Category**.
- **Pickup**: one of the community's pickup places, or **Elsewhere** with a
  city.
- **Description**: optional, up to 5000 characters. Simple formatting
  works: `**bold**`, `*italic*`, lists, links and line breaks. Headings,
  images and HTML show as plain text.
- **Photos**: up to five, JPEG, PNG, HEIC or AVIF (as phones save them), 10 MB each. They are resized and
  their location and camera data removed before anyone sees them. The
  first one is the cover shown on the board.

If your community has a chat channel connected (Slack, Mattermost, Discord
or IRC), a new post is announced there a few minutes after you publish
it: its title, price or kind, category and pickup, with a link. Your name
is not in it.

## Your name

If you sign in by email, your name starts as your address. Choose your name
in the header, then **Settings**: it is what other members see on your
listings and messages. With a sign-in service such as Google, your name
comes from there.

## Your language

Jumble speaks your community's language until you choose another in
**Settings**: English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese,
Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish or Dutch. Your pages and the emails
sent to you are then in it; what other members write stays as they wrote it.

## Your data

**Privacy**, at the bottom of every page, says what your community's
Jumble keeps about you and why.

**Settings** → **Download your data** gives you a file (JSON) with
everything Jumble holds about you: your account, listings, favorites,
conversations and the reports you made.

**Delete your account**, after confirming, deletes your listings and their
photos, your favorites and settings, and signs you out. Messages you sent
stay with the people you wrote to, shown from "A former member"; they can
no longer reply. Signing in again later starts a new, empty account. Your
community's backups keep what was deleted for a while (usually up to three
months) before it is gone from them too.

## Your listings

**My listings** shows all of yours, whatever their state. On a listing's
page you can:

- **Edit** it while it is on the board, including removing photos or adding
  more (five in all).
- **Mark as reserved** when it is promised to someone: it stays on the
  board, marked **Reserved**, and others can still message you in case it
  falls through. **Make available again** undoes it.
- **Mark as sold** (sales), **Mark as given away** (free items) or **Mark
  as found** (wanted posts): it
  leaves the board and search but stays visible to anyone with its link,
  marked as such. **Put back on the board** undoes it.
- **Remove listing**: deletes it and its photos for good, after asking you
  to confirm. Conversations about it stay, saying it was removed.

A listing two months on the board without news from you (no edit, no
change of status) gets you an email asking whether it is still available;
your community may wait longer, or never ask. Listings to lend are never
asked about. Answer
on its page with **Yes, keep it on the board**, or mark it as gone. Without
an answer within 15 days it leaves the board, marked **Expired**; **Put
back on the board** on its page brings it back.

## Saved searches

After searching, or choosing a type or a category, **Save this search**
keeps it (ten at most). Once a day at most, Jumble emails you the new
listings that match your saved searches, when there are any. **Your saved
searches**, under the board's title, lists them, each a link to its
results, and lets you remove them.

## Favorites

The heart on someone else's listing, or on its card on the board, adds it
to your **Favorites** (in the header); pressing it again takes it out.
Favorites are yours alone: nobody else, the owner included, sees who saved
what or how many did. Sold and given-away listings stay there, marked; a
removed listing leaves. When a favorite is reserved, sold, given away or
back on the board, **Favorites** in the header shows how many changed, and
the page marks them **Changed** until you have seen them.

## Messages

**Message** (followed by the owner's name) on someone else's listing starts
a conversation about it. The subject starts as the listing's title; change
it if you like. Writing again about the same listing continues the same
conversation.

**Messages** in the header lists your conversations, latest first, with a
count of unread messages. Opening a conversation marks what you see as
read. Conversations are private between the two of you; nobody else,
moderators included, can read them in Jumble.

If a message stays unread for five minutes, Jumble emails it to you, with a
link back. Several messages in a row lead to one email, and at most one an
hour per conversation. To stop these emails, choose your name in the header
and turn them off in **Settings**.

To be told at once, turn on notifications for a device: in **Settings**,
**Notify me on this device**, then allow them when the browser asks. A
notification says who wrote and about what, not the message itself, and
opens the conversation. Turn them on on each device you want them on; the
list under it shows them all, and **Remove** stops them for one you no
longer have. On an iPhone or iPad, add Jumble to your home screen first
(Share, then **Add to Home Screen**) and turn them on from there.

## The weekly email

Once a week, Jumble emails you what others posted on the board that week:
up to twenty listings, newest first, with a link to the rest. A week with
nothing new sends nothing. Turn it off in **Settings**.

## Reporting a listing

**Report this listing**, under a listing, tells the community's moderators it
does not belong here. Saying why is optional but helps. You can report a
listing once; the moderators see who reported it.

## Appearance

The sun or moon in the header switches between light and dark. Jumble
follows your system's choice until you pick one.

## For moderators

Moderators are named in Jumble's configuration by email; there is no
moderator setting in the app itself.

**Removing any listing.** On someone else's listing, moderators see
**Remove listing (moderation)**. After confirming, the listing and its
photos are deleted and its owner is emailed; what you write in the reason
field goes in that email.

**Reports.** When a listing is reported for the first time, moderators get
an email. **Manage** in the header, with the number of listings
waiting, leads to the reported listings, the longest waiting first, each
with who reported it, when and why. For each one:

- **Remove listing**: as above, the reason going to the owner.
- **Dismiss**: keeps the listing and closes the reports.

The moderators' pages link to each other at the top: **Reports**, **Who may
sign in**, **Categories**, **Pickup places**, **Appearance** and **Poster**.

**Categories and pickup places.** Members file listings under a category,
and choose a pickup place or type a city. On their pages, moderators
**Rename** an entry (its listings follow), move it **Up** or **Down** in
the order members see, **Retire** it, or add one. A retired entry is no
longer offered, but its listings keep it, and **Restore** offers it again;
editing such a listing asks for a new choice. There is always at least one
category; with no pickup places, members always type a city. A new
Jumble starts with a set of general categories, in its language, and no
places.

**Who may sign in.** Unless anyone can sign in, **Who may sign in** lists
the addresses and patterns from the configuration, and
those moderators added. Add an address (`alice@gmail.com`), a domain
(`*@partner.org`) or its subdomains (`*@*.partner.org`), one per line; tick
**Email an invitation** to let the addresses know. **Withdraw** takes
effect at once: people only it let in are signed out at their next click,
and their accounts stay.

**Join link.** Unless anyone can sign in, **Who may sign in** also offers a
join link: whoever opens it can join with any address, by email or the
sign-in provider, without being on the list. Their address is then added,
marked as joined with the join link, and can be withdrawn like any other.
There is one link at a time, working until the date chosen or for good.
Anyone holding it can join, so share it where you would invite people in
person; **Renew** makes a new one and stops the earlier one at once, and
**Turn off the join link** stops it altogether. A removed member cannot
come back through it.

**Remove a member** takes their email address and, after asking, deletes
their account for good, with their listings and photos, as when they
delete it themselves; their messages stay, from a former member. They can
never sign in again, whatever lets their address in. Moderators are set in
the configuration and are removed there.

**Poster.** A page to print for a noticeboard, by the coffee machine or the
lift: the community's name or logo, what it is for, and a QR code that
opens it, its address written out below. **Print** prints the poster alone;
the links beside it print it in any of Jumble's languages. While there is a
join link, the code can open it instead, so that whoever scans the poster
can join: anyone who sees the poster then can.

**Appearance.** The community's colours, for every page and everyone:
Forest, Plum, Ink, Slate, or one after an editor theme (Tokyo Night,
Catppuccin, Gruvbox, Nord, Rosé Pine, Solarized, Dracula, One, GitHub,
Everforest, Kanagawa), each shown light and dark. **Save** applies it at once. Each palette
has a light and a dark side; members see the one they chose, or their
device's.

Every removal is kept as a record, with who removed what and why, whether
it was reported or not. A later report on a listing whose reports were
dismissed starts again.

Moderators cannot read conversations.
