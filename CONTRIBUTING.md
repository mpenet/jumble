# Contributing

Thank you for helping. Bug reports, questions and suggestions are welcome
as GitHub issues.

## Changes

Open an issue first for anything beyond a small fix, so that we agree on
the approach before you spend time on it. Then:

- follow the code's style, and add tests for what you change (see
  [the development guide](doc/development.md));
- keep the unit tests, the integration tests and the linter passing.

## Contributor license agreement

Jumble is offered under the [AGPL](LICENSE), and under a commercial license
to organisations the AGPL does not suit. For that to stay possible, every
contribution is made under the [contributor license agreement](CLA.md): you
keep the copyright of your work and grant Jumble's maintainer a license to
distribute it under either.

State your agreement once, in your first pull request's description:

> I have read the Jumble Contributor License Agreement (CLA.md) and agree
> to it for this and my future contributions to Jumble.
