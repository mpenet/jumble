Small on purpose.

Big marketplaces are made for strangers. Jumble is made for people who already share a workplace, a campus or a street, and sort the rest out face to face.

i.

Among people you know

Only your community signs in: with a link sent by email, or the accounts it already has, such as Google Workspace or Keycloak.

ii.

Nothing in between

No payments, fees, ratings or ads. People agree on a price, a place, a day to bring the ladder back, and meet.

iii.

Yours to keep

One Jumble is one community, on its own server, under its own name and logo, its data in one file backed up as it changes.

What it does.

The features a community board needs, and not many more.

Sell, give away, lend

A title, a few lines, up to five photos, a category, a price when it is for sale, and where to pick it up.

Browse and search

Newest first, filtered by kind and category, searched as you type. Every view is a link to share.

Private messages

Write to whoever posted it; the conversation stays with the listing. Missed messages come by email.

Saved searches and favorites

Save “bike” and hear about new ones each day. Keep listings at hand with a heart nobody else sees.

Moderation

Members report what does not belong. Moderators review it on one page, remove or dismiss, and remove members if need be.

Your data, your call

Download everything Jumble holds about you, or delete your account in a click.

In English, French and German

Your community’s name and logo on every page, in its language and time zone.

Light, fast, for everyone

Works without JavaScript and better with it. Light and dark themes, labelled forms, keyboard friendly.

From a lamp in the cellar to a lamp on a desk.

Run your own.

One Jumble is one community, on a small server: one process, one database file, photos and backups in object storage. Up and running in minutes: the scripts provided do everything.

  • A small serverUbuntu or Debian, made ready by the scripts. One CPU and 2 GB of memory are plenty.
  • A domainJumble gets its HTTPS certificate from Let’s Encrypt, or use your own proxy.
  • Object storageTwo S3-compatible buckets, for photos and for backups.
  • EmailAny SMTP account, or an OpenID Connect provider to sign in with.

Made with Clojure, SQLite, Datastar, Litestream and libvips.

# server, buckets and keys, at once
SSH_KEY=my-key deploy/provision.sh exoscale my-club

# your settings: who may sign in, email
$EDITOR deploy/instances/my-club/jumble.env

# build, ship and start it, HTTPS included
INSTANCE=deploy/instances/my-club deploy/deploy.sh

# or any server of yours, any S3 storage
HOST=root@your-server deploy/bootstrap.sh

Every change is copied to the backup bucket within a second; a lost server is restored from it on the next start. The deployment guide takes you through it.
